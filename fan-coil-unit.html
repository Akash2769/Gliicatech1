How It Works

Three-Step Cooling Cycle

01

Warm Air Pulled In

Fans draw warm air from inside the electrical enclosure and pass it across the chilled-water coil.

02

Heat Transferred to Water

The coil absorbs heat from the air into the circulating chilled water — no refrigerant compressor required inside the unit.

03

Cooled Air Returned

Cool, dry air is circulated back into the enclosure. All extracted heat leaves via the water outlet — zero hot air to the room.

In situations where chilled water is already running in the system, or where there is very limited space for a standard Panel AC, Gliicatech Water-to-Air Heat Exchangers can be used to transfer heat from the panel to the chilled water circulating in the coil.

There are no filters to be cleaned and no hot air expelled to the environment. These systems are ideal for contaminated and dusty environments. In-house chilled water can be used, or a separate chiller can be installed to suit different applications.
