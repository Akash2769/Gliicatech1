About Panel AC

Built for Industrial-Grade Enclosure Protection

Gliicatech specialises in heavy-duty air conditioners designed to protect your enclosures from heat, dust, and humidity — ensuring long life and reliability for your critical electronic equipment.

🔄
Dual Air Circuit
Internal circuit circulates cool air inside the panel; external circuit dissipates extracted heat to the atmosphere. Zero cross-contamination.
🌡️
Auto Temperature Controller
LED/LCD display with auto-cut and auto-start technology. Optional high/low temperature sound alarm at no extra cost.
🔩
Hermetic Compressor Technology
Reciprocating, rotary and scroll technology options. Standard-model fans with easily available spare parts.
🏗️
Rugged Enclosure Options
MS powder-coated, GI powder-coated, or full SS-304 body for pharma and food-packaging industries. IP55 standard protection.
💧
Condensate Drain System
Drain pipe removes condensate/moisture from the panel. Extension pipe provided for flexible routing.
⚙️

Customisable units available — drop-in replacements for imported Panel ACs. Custom dimensions, voltages, body material, and special configurations on request.
